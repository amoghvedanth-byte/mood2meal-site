Your account

Delete your account

You can delete your Mood2Meal account at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Mood2Meal and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.
  4. Enter your password when asked. If you signed up with Sign in with Apple, there is no password step.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including your email address and display name.
  • Your saved meals, Fine-Tune Presets, shopping list and cooking history.
  • Your preferences, including dietary needs and allergies.
  • The Mood2Meal data stored on the device you delete from.

What is not affected

  • Your subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel Premium, because Apple bills it. Cancel it first in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • Anonymous crash reports. These are not linked to your account, so they can't be matched to you or removed with it.

Can't get into the app?

Email support@mood2meal.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm when it's done, within 30 days.
