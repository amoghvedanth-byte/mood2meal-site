How it works

From "what's for dinner?" to cooking in a minute.

Set up takes about 30 seconds. After that, it's three steps.

  1. Say how you feel

    Pick your mood and how much effort you have in you tonight.

  2. Get one meal

    Mood2Meal suggests a meal that fits the moment, your diet and your kitchen. Not feeling it? Ask for another.

  3. Cook it, step by step

    Follow clear steps with amounts and timers. Missing an ingredient? The recipe can adjust.
