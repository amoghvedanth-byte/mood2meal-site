Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Mood2Meal respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Who Is Responsible

The data controller for Mood2Meal is Amogh Vedantha Krishna, Halmstad, Sweden. For any privacy question or request, email support@mood2meal.com.

What We Collect

  • Account info — Email address and display name, stored securely in Supabase.
  • Preferences — Dietary needs, allergies, cuisine preferences and the kitchen equipment you own. If you have an account, these are kept with it so they follow you to a new phone; as a guest they stay on your device.
  • Usage data — Recipes you save, cook or skip (your cook history), the feedback you give on a recipe after cooking it, and any recipe you report (with the optional note you write). Used to avoid repeating meals you've just seen, to show your Cook History, and to keep the recipe library accurate and safe.
  • Things you create — Your Fine-Tune Presets and your shopping list, kept with your account.
  • Purchase information — Your subscription status, managed by RevenueCat and Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Crash & diagnostic data — Anonymous crash reports via Sentry so we can fix bugs. You can turn this off any time in Settings › Crash & Error Reporting.
  • No advertising or tracking — No ad SDKs, no cross-app tracking, and we never sell your data to anyone.

How We Use Your Data

  • To generate personalised meal recommendations via AI.
  • To keep your preferences, saved meals, presets, shopping list and cooking history with your account, so they are there on any phone you sign in on.
  • To process your subscription (if you choose Premium).
  • To diagnose and fix crashes (only if crash reporting is enabled).

Legal Basis

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your data on these grounds:

  • To provide the service you asked for — your account, preferences, saved meals, recommendations and subscription.
  • Your consent — the dietary needs and allergies you choose to enter, which may reveal information about your health. You can withdraw consent at any time by removing them in the app or deleting your account.
  • Our legitimate interest in a working app — anonymous crash reports, which you can switch off in Settings.

AI Recipe Generation

Most meals come from our own recipe library. When nothing in it fits, your mood selection and food preferences (such as dietary needs, allergies, and available equipment) are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to create a new recipe. This data is not used to train AI models. We do not send your name, email, or account details with these requests. A recipe created this way is checked and then added to the shared library, where other people may be offered it; nothing about you is attached to it.

Data Storage & Security

  • Account data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS in Stockholm, Sweden) with Row-Level Security.
  • For signed-in users, preferences, cook history, presets and the shopping list are stored with the account in Supabase, readable only by you. Signing out removes them from the phone. Guests' data stays on their device.
  • All network communication uses HTTPS encryption.

Transfers Outside the EU

Your account data and crash reports are stored in the EU. Some of our service providers are based in the United States: Anthropic (recipe generation), RevenueCat (subscription status) and Apple (billing). Where data is sent to them, it is protected by safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How Long We Keep Data

  • Account data, preferences, saved meals, presets, shopping list and history are kept until you delete your account, and are then removed.
  • Anonymous crash reports are kept for no longer than 90 days.
  • Requests sent for AI recipe generation contain no account details and are not kept by us.

Your Rights

  • Access — Settings › What We Store lists what we keep. For a copy of your own data, use Settings › Request a copy of my data, or email us.
  • Delete — Delete your account and all associated data in Settings › Delete Account.
  • Portability — We send your data as a file you can keep or take elsewhere. Ask through Settings › Request a copy of my data, or email us.
  • Opt out — Disable crash reporting in Settings › Crash & Error Reporting.
  • Correct, restrict or object — You can ask us to correct your data, or to limit or stop a particular use of it.
  • Complain — You can complain to your local data protection authority. In Sweden this is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

To use any of these rights, email support@mood2meal.com. We reply within one month.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database & authentication
  • Anthropic Claude — AI recipe generation (prompts are not used for model training)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management & receipt validation
  • Sentry — crash & error reporting (optional, can be disabled in Settings)
  • Apple — subscription billing and Sign in with Apple

Children

Mood2Meal is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated through the app.
